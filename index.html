AULA, an interior design studio

We design apartments, houses, restaurants and commercial spaces, and we stay with the project until the last lamp is hung: design project, author supervision on site, and furnishing with real suppliers.

Our interiors are calm, warm and built to last: natural materials, deliberate light and storage that disappears. Every project starts with a conversation about how you live or work. Tell us about yours.
